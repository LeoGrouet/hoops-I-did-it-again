### Ideas for the future features:
- Add a "Teams" tab to display the different teams and their players.
- Implement a "Statistics" tab to show player and team statistics.
- Teams chat: Allow team members to communicate with each other through a chat feature.
- Notifications: Implement a notification system to alert users about upcoming games, team updates, or chat messages.
- User profiles: Allow users to create and customize their profiles, including their favorite teams and players.
- Game highlights: Add a feature to display highlights from recent games, such as top plays or player performances.
- Social media integration: Allow users to share their favorite games, teams, or players on social media platforms.
- Leaderboards: Create leaderboards to rank teams and players based on their performance in games.
